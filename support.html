PurlTally Support

PurlTally is a personal project by an individual developer.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
On your device. PurlTally does not have a server and does not transmit any data.
How do I move my data to a new phone?
Export a backup from Settings, then import it on the new device.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Cancel from your store's subscription settings. PurlTally cannot cancel subscriptions on your behalf.
How do I restore a purchase?
Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, or tap Restore on the paywall.
The app shows a blank paywall.
This usually means the store is not reachable. Check your connection and try again.
