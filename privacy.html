PurlTally Privacy Policy

Effective: October 19, 2026

PurlTally is published by Amir ShahMoradi, an individual developer in Los Angeles, California, USA.

Contact: mehdishahradi@yahoo.com

Data we collect

None. PurlTally does not collect, store on any server, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDK that sends data.

Data on your device

Your projects, counters, notes, and any photos or PDFs you choose to add are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. You can delete them at any time from within the app, or by uninstalling the app.

Android cloud backup is disabled for this app. On iOS, the app's container may be included in the user's own iCloud backup, which is controlled entirely by the user and by Apple. The developer has no access to it.

Permissions

  • Photo library / camera: used only when you choose to attach an image to a project. Photos are stored on your device only.
  • Notifications: used only for reminders you set yourself. Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification content is transmitted.

No other permissions are used.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. The developer never receives your payment information. PurlTally reads the purchase status locally to unlock Pro features.

Age signal

Starting January 1, 2027, California law (AB 1043) requires apps to request an age range signal from the platform. PurlTally reads this signal on-device and stores only a coarse bracket (adult, minor, or unknown). The signal is never transmitted and is never written to any backup file. If the signal indicates a minor, the only effect is that the paywall is hidden; no functionality is withheld.

Children

PurlTally is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data from anyone. The app is rated 4+ on the App Store and Everyone on Google Play.

California residents

Because PurlTally collects no personal information, there is nothing to sell or share as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

International users

PurlTally is offered only in the United States. There is no server and no data transfer, so data-residency and cross-border-transfer rules do not apply.

Changes

Any update to this policy will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.

Contact
